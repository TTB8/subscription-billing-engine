# Code of conduct

This project follows the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
(CC BY 4.0), summarized here.

## Our standard

Be respectful and constructive. In issues, pull requests and discussions:

- Be kind to other people, and assume good faith.
- Criticize code and ideas, not people.
- Give and accept feedback gracefully; explain your reasoning.
- Respect that people have different backgrounds and experience.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, insults or personal attacks,
discriminatory language, publishing other people's private information, and
anything else that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit, hide or remove comments, issues and contributions that
do not follow this code, and may restrict participation for repeated or serious
violations. To report a problem, contact the maintainer through the profile at
<https://github.com/TTB8>. Reports are handled privately.

This code applies in all project spaces and when someone represents the project
in public.
